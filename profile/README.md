# GuignaLabs

**Small by nature. Built for complexity.**

Independent software engineering lab.

We design, build, and experiment with software, data, and infrastructure.
Our focus is practical tools with clear architecture and room to evolve.

## What we're building

Our first project is a modular platform for sports venues: gyms, studios,
courts, pools, and mixed facilities.

We're starting with business administration—memberships, payments, scheduling,
and facility operations. Personal training logs and performance tracking are
planned for a later stage. The platform is in early development.

## How we work

- Understand the problem before expanding the system.
- Keep modules focused and data ownership explicit.
- Test real workflows, including failures and recovery.
- Start small and evolve with evidence.
